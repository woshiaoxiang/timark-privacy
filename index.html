Timark

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

Timark is an iOS app that helps you organize items, set countdowns, and receive reminders.

Your privacy matters. Timark is designed to keep your item information on your device, without requiring an account or uploading your content to our servers.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Timark stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Photos you take within the app, including thumbnails and temporary capture drafts.
  • Item names, custom text, and categories.
  • Countdown dates, durations, and reminder settings.
  • App preferences and information needed to maintain your items.

This information is used to provide Timark's core features.

Timark does not upload this content to developer-operated servers.

2. Camera Access

Timark requests access to your camera so you can photograph items and replace existing item photos.

Photos captured in Timark are stored in the app's local storage.

Timark does not require access to your photo library or microphone for these features.

3. Notifications

With your permission, Timark schedules local notifications for countdown reminders.

Notifications may include item names, custom text, or photo thumbnails. Depending on your iOS notification settings, this information may appear on your Lock Screen.

You can manage notification permissions and preview visibility through iOS Settings.

4. Data Collection and Tracking

Timark does not currently:

  • Require user registration or a Timark account.
  • Collect or transmit your item content to developer-operated servers.
  • Use advertising trackers.
  • Use third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Sell your personal data.

Timark's core item-management features operate locally on your device.

5. In-App Purchases

Timark may offer optional one-time purchases through Apple's In-App Purchase system.

Purchases, payment processing, and purchase restoration are handled through Apple and StoreKit.

Timark does not directly collect your payment card information. Apple's processing of purchase-related information is governed by Apple's applicable privacy practices.

6. Device Backups

Although Timark does not provide its own cloud synchronization service, app data may be included in backups managed by iOS, iCloud, or your computer, depending on your device settings.

Those backups are managed through Apple's systems and your selected backup configuration.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your item information remains stored locally while you use Timark, unless you delete or modify it.

You can delete individual items within the app. When an item or draft is removed, Timark performs local cleanup of associated records and image files. Some cleanup operations may be completed later if an earlier attempt fails.

Uninstalling Timark generally removes its locally stored app data, subject to any existing device backups and applicable iOS behavior.

Timark does not maintain a developer-operated account or server-side copy of your item content for you to request deletion of.

8. Your Choices

You can:

  • Allow or deny camera access in iOS Settings.
  • Allow or deny notifications and manage notification previews.
  • Edit or delete items stored within Timark.
  • Remove the app from your device.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Timark's features or data practices change.

The updated policy will be published at the same public Privacy Policy URL, with a revised effective date when appropriate.
